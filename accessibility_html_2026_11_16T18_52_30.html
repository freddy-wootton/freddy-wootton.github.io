Accessibility Statement

Last updated: November 2026

1. Commitment

I want this site to be usable by as many people as possible, regardless of ability, device, or assistive technology. I aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA standard.

2. What I Have Done

  • Used semantic HTML landmarks (<header>, <main>, <nav>, <footer>) for clear page structure
  • Provided a “Skip to main content” link
  • Ensured all interactive elements are keyboard-accessible with visible focus indicators
  • Maintained colour contrast of at least 4.5:1 for body text and 3:1 for large text
  • Included descriptive alt text for all meaningful images
  • Labelled all form fields with associated <label> elements
  • Used aria-live regions for dynamic status messages (e.g. form submission feedback)
  • Respected prefers-reduced-motion — animations are disabled for users who prefer reduced motion
  • Supported both light and dark colour schemes
  • Used relative font sizes so text scales with browser settings
  • Ensured no content or meaning is conveyed by colour alone

3. Known Limitations

  • Skill proficiency bars: These are visual indicators. The equivalent text label and ARIA progressbar role are provided, but the visual gradient may not convey nuance to all users.
  • Third-party form service: The contact form is processed by Formspree. While I have made the form itself accessible, I cannot guarantee the accessibility of Formspree’s server-side processing or confirmation pages.

4. Feedback and Contact

If you find an accessibility barrier on this site, I would genuinely like to know so I can fix it. Please contact me through the contact form or via LinkedIn (opens in new tab), and I will do my best to address the issue promptly.

5. Technical Details

This site is built with semantic HTML5, CSS custom properties, and vanilla JavaScript. It has been tested with keyboard navigation and screen-reader software. It is designed to work across modern browsers including Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge on both desktop and mobile.
