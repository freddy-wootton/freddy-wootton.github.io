Cookie Notice

Last updated: November 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your web browser. They are widely used to make websites work, improve user experience, and provide information to site owners. The Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003 (PECR) govern their use in the UK.

2. Cookies This Site Uses

This site does not set any cookies. There are no analytics cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party tracking cookies.

Local Storage

This site uses your browser’s localStorage (not a cookie) to remember your light/dark theme preference. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to any server. You can clear it at any time via your browser settings.

localStorage for theme preference is considered “strictly necessary” for the functionality you have requested (choosing a display theme), and does not require consent under PECR.

3. Third-Party Cookies

No third-party cookies are set by this site. If embedded content is added in future (e.g. a video or interactive demo), this notice will be updated and consent will be obtained before any non-essential cookies are set.

4. No Consent Banner

Because this site does not set any non-essential cookies or use non-essential local storage, a cookie consent banner is not required under PECR. If this changes, a consent mechanism will be added before any non-essential cookies are introduced — with no dark patterns.

5. Managing Cookies

You can control and delete cookies (and localStorage) through your browser settings. For guidance, visit aboutcookies.org (opens in new tab).

6. Changes

If I add analytics or any feature that uses cookies, I will update this notice and add a consent mechanism before doing so.
