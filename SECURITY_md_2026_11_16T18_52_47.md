
# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a security vulnerability on this site, I would appreciate
responsible disclosure. Please **do not** open a public GitHub issue.

**How to report:**

1. Contact me via the [contact form](https://freddy-wootton.github.io/#contact) or
   [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/freddy-wootton-381151210).
2. Include:
   - A description of the vulnerability
   - Steps to reproduce it
   - The potential impact
3. I will acknowledge your report within **7 days** and aim to resolve
   confirmed issues promptly.

## Scope

This policy covers:
- The personal portfolio website hosted at `freddy-wootton.github.io`
- The source code in this repository

## Out of Scope

- Third-party services (GitHub Pages infrastructure, Formspree)
- Social engineering, phishing, or denial-of-service attacks

## Recognition

I am happy to credit responsible reporters (with your permission) in the
ATTRIBUTIONS file.
